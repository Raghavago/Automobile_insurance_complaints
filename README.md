# Automobile Insurance Complaints Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes automobile insurance complaint rankings and premium trends for **Allstate Insurance Company** using data from the **New York State Department of Financial Services (DFS)**.

The analysis focuses on Allstate's complaint patterns from **2009–2015** and compares its performance with four major insurance companies: **GEICO General, State Farm, Progressive, and Liberty Mutual**.

The objective is to understand complaint trends, complaint categories, complaint ratios, premium trends, and the relative performance of different insurance companies.

## Dataset

**Data Source:** New York State Department of Financial Services (DFS)

**Filing Years:** 2009–2016

The analysis uses **2009–2015 as the effective trend period** because the 2016 records in the dataset are exact duplicates of the 2015 records. Therefore, 2016 was not treated as a separate observation.

## Companies Analyzed

## Complaint Categories

The complaints were analyzed using three DFS categories:

* **Upheld**
* **Question of Fact**
* **Not Upheld / Withdrawn**

For Allstate, Question-of-Fact complaints represented the largest category, accounting for **38.1%** of complaints during 2009–2015.

## Key Findings

### Allstate Complaint Trends

Allstate's total complaints decreased substantially from **1,023 in 2009 to 237 in 2015**, representing a **76.8% decrease**.

The complaint ratio also decreased from **0.1418 in 2009 to 0.0308 in 2015**, a **78.3% decrease**.

The year 2010 recorded the highest number of complaints, with **1,350 complaints**, largely driven by a significant increase in Question-of-Fact cases.

### Premium Trend

Allstate's premiums written declined from approximately **$1,269.4 million in 2009 to $747.4 million in 2015**, representing a **41.1% decrease**.

The improvement in complaint ratio therefore occurred alongside a shrinking premium base.

### 2015 Company Comparison

| Company        | Complaint Ratio | Total Complaints | Premiums ($M) |

| GEICO General  |          0.0109 |              503 |      2,199.89 |
| State Farm     |          0.0314 |              221 |      1,434.02 |

| Liberty Mutual |          0.0164 |               56 |        304.59 |

Progressive and GEICO General recorded the lowest complaint ratios among the five companies in 2015.

Allstate and State Farm had comparatively higher complaint ratios.

## Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the major improvement area for Allstate is reducing **Question-of-Fact complaints**.

Recommended actions include:

* Improve claims documentation.
* Provide clearer communication during claim resolution.
* Reduce ambiguity in no-fault claim decisions.
* Improve the speed of resolving disputed claims.
* Continue monitoring complaint ratios against competitors.

## Conclusion

The analysis shows that Allstate's New York automobile insurance complaint experience improved significantly between 2009 and 2015. However, its complaint ratio remained higher than GEICO General and Progressive in 2015.

The analysis also indicates that the improvement occurred alongside a significant reduction in Allstate's premium base. Therefore, reducing Question-of-Fact complaints and improving claims communication and resolution should remain key areas of focus.
